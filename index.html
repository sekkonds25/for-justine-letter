Dear Justine

I have something I wanted to tell you.

Dear Justine,

A little something I wanted to put into words.

I think part of why I’m writing this instead of just trying to say it casually is because I don’t want to leave you guessing about why I’ve been acting differently. I could’ve just kept quiet and let things go back to normal, but I realized I’d rather be honest about what’s been going through my head than become mysterious or make you try to figure it out yourself. This isn’t me asking you to fix anything or give me an answer. I just feel like if these thoughts are something I’ve been carrying, I’d rather share them with you than let my silence speak for me.

I’ve been thinking about this for the past few days, and I think I finally understand something about myself that I want to share with you. Hindi naman siya something na kailangan mong ayusin or kailangan mong sagutin. I just feel like it would be better if you knew where I was coming from, especially kung napansin mo rin na medyo iba ako these past few days.

I actually took the liberty of making this into a letter kasi alam kong mahaba siya para sa normal na chat or message. I also didn’t want to just send everything right away habang magulo pa yung thoughts ko. I took some time to write this out properly, partly because I want to explain myself as clearly as I can and lessen the chance na may ma-misinterpret or mawala sa context yung gusto kong sabihin. I think I’d rather let you read everything in full kaysa magbitaw ako ng ilang messages and leave you trying to figure out what I actually mean.

And part din ng reason why I’m writing this is because I don’t want my silence or the way I’ve been acting lately to become something mysterious for you to figure out. I could’ve just kept quiet and let things go back to normal, but I realized I’d rather share what’s been going through my head than leave you guessing. This is really just my side of things—what I felt, what I realized, and where I’m at now.

After Thursday, my first instinct was to pull away muna. I think yung nangyari that day pushed me into that reaction too, especially because it was the first time I actually asked someone in person like that. I don't really have much experience with that kind of situation, so I think I was caught off guard by how much I ended up feeling afterward. Instead of immediately talking about it, automatic reaction ko was to step back muna. I needed some space to let myself cool down and understand what I was actually feeling instead of saying something while I was still all over the place. And I think that space was good for me.

Pero habang ginagawa ko yun, may isa rin akong napansin nitong buong week. Kahit sinusubukan kong mag-step back and regulate myself, somehow nagkakausap pa rin tayo. May mga pagkakataon na work-related, may mga pagkakataon na napupunta pa rin sa jokes or random things, and eventually I realized na hindi ko pala kayang pilitin yung sarili ko na maging distant sayo just because I was trying to protect myself. Siguro marupok lang talaga ako HAHAHA, pero more than that, narealize ko na hindi pala yun yung gusto kong maging way ng pakikitungo sayo.

And honestly, yung joke natin about "unconfessing" added another layer to what I was already processing. Hindi naman sa kinuha ko siya as something serious, pero dahil medyo vulnerable na rin ako that time, it got into my head a little. Napaisip ako, and I think that's where some of my fear came from. Not because I wanted to assume anything from that joke, but because I realized na takot pala ako sa possibility na bawiin ko lahat ng nasabi at pinakita ko just because I became afraid.

And I think that's something I don't want to do. I don't want fear to make me suddenly take back what I genuinely feel, or make me act like I don't care just because caring makes me vulnerable. I know myself enough to know that when I care about someone, I naturally want to reach out, listen, check in, share things, and spend time with them. That's part of who I am, and I don't want to shrink that part of myself just to make things feel safer for me.

Siguro kasi habang iniisip ko rin kung ano ba talaga yung gusto ko, narealize ko na I don't really want something na puro sweetness or yung kailangan laging may romantic gesture. I don't even really know what a relationship is supposed to look like for me because, to be honest, I haven't been in one. Pero alam ko na gusto ko yung may tropa vibe sa loob nun. Yung comfortable tayo, nagtatawanan, nagra-rant, nag-aasaran, nag-aaya bigla, and we can just exist around each other without everything having to feel romantic.

And maybe that's also why I appreciated it when you called me "tropa." I know it can just be a simple word, and I'm not trying to give it some deeper meaning than you intended. But I think it described something I genuinely value about the connection we have. Yung comfortable, makulit, nag-aasaran, nagra-rant, and hindi kailangan laging maging sweet or romantic.

I think that's the kind of foundation I'd want to have with someone I eventually have a relationship with. I don't know exactly what that relationship would look like because I haven't experienced one myself, but I know I want the person I'm with to also feel like my best friend and teammate. Someone I can grow with, be vulnerable with, laugh with, and eventually love deeply.

And if I'm being honest, I know I want to explore that possibility with you.

That's also why I want to pursue you intentionally. Hindi dahil kailangan kong may title tayo, or because I want to rush anything. I just genuinely like having you in my life, and I want to know you beyond the parts of us that work allows.

Yung time natin before sa Megamall is probably one of the reasons why I keep wanting to do that. I really enjoyed that time with you. It felt different from our usual conversations at work. We got to talk about random things, personal stuff, joke around, and just let the day pass. And I think that's really what I'm looking for whenever I ask you to hang out.

And I also want to acknowledge something about the way I've been inviting you lately. Minsan I get these ideas in my head and I get excited about them, and before I know it, parang naging decision ko na rin for you. Yung time na sinabi ko na sasabayan kita and even said na maghihintay ako, I realize now na I might have made it feel less like an invitation and more like something you were already expected to go along with. I'm sorry for that.

Hindi naman talaga yun yung intention ko. I genuinely just wanted to include you in the things I was already thinking of doing. Kaya nga may mga "timezone" invites ako minsan. It's basically me thinking, "this sounds fun, gusto ko siyang makasama dito." But I don't want my excitement about spending time with you to become pressure on you. I can invite you without assuming your answer, and that's something I want to be more mindful of.

Hindi rin dahil iniisip kong kailangan mong magsaya or kailangan kitang ilabas kasi pagod ka. I don't want to decide that for you. Gusto ko lang talaga makasama ka. If we both had a long week and we can spend a few hours laughing, eating, walking around, or just talking nonsense, then I'd enjoy that. And I'd like to share that with you.

And when I say I want to pursue you, I'm not pursuing you simply because I want a girlfriend or because I want a relationship title. The title isn't really the thing I'm chasing. What I want is the person and everything that can naturally grow from knowing each other.

I think I'd want something that starts from that kind of comfort and friendship, where two people can actually get to know each other without having to force a label or decide immediately what they're supposed to become. Someone you can laugh with, be vulnerable with, support, grow with, and eventually see where things naturally go.

And I think that's part of what I mean when I say I want to pursue you intentionally. Hindi ko naman kailangan malaman ngayon kung saan siya pupunta. I just know that I genuinely enjoy getting to know you, and I want to give myself the chance to explore that without forcing either of us into an outcome.

At the same time, I know that being honest about what I want doesn't mean I get to decide what you want. You have your own choices, your own pace, and your own boundaries. I want to be able to be honest about my feelings while still giving you the freedom to choose what feels right for you.

That's probably the biggest thing I realized from taking that space this week. I can choose myself without having to become distant from you. I can protect myself without pretending that I don't care. And I can be honest about wanting you in my life without expecting you to give me something in return.

So ayun. Hindi ko sinusulat 'to para kailangan mong sagutin lahat ng sinabi ko. I don't need you to figure everything out right now, and I don't need an answer just because I've finally said all of this. If someday there's something you want to say about it, I'll listen. But you don't owe me a response to this message.

I just wanted to let this out properly instead of keeping it in my head and letting my silence speak for me. I wanted you to know why I became distant for a while, what I realized during that space, and where I'm at now.

I still want to pursue you intentionally. I don't regret saying that, and I don't regret caring about you. I also don't want my pursuit to become something you feel trapped by. I want to be able to care for you, invite you, spend time with you, and be honest with you while still respecting whatever pace and boundaries you have.

For now, that's really all I wanted to say.

Ayun lang. Sorry ang haba HAHAHA. I think I just needed to finally put this into words instead of letting my brain write the story for me. You don't have to figure everything out right now. You don't even have to say anything. I just wanted you to know me a little more clearly.

— Emil
