Dear Justine

I have something I wanted to tell you.

Dear Justine,

A little something I wanted to put into words.

Okay, so I guess this is the modern version of writing someone a letter hahaha. Instead of papel at ballpen, gumawa pa talaga ako ng sariling digital version nito.

I know this is probably a lot for a normal chat, and I didn't really want to just throw a wall of messages at you habang magulo pa yung thoughts ko. So I took some time to actually think things through properly and write this out for you.

More than anything, I just don't want you to be left guessing about why I've been acting differently. I could've just kept quiet and let things go back to normal, but that didn't feel right to me.

And just to be clear from the start, this isn't me asking you to fix anything or give me an answer. This is really just my side of things—what I felt, what I realized, and what I want you to know.

After Thursday, my first instinct was to pull away muna. I think yung nangyari that day pushed me into that reaction too, especially because it was the first time I actually asked someone in person like that. I don’t really have much experience with that kind of situation, so I think I was caught off guard by how much I ended up feeling afterward. Instead of immediately talking about it, automatic reaction ko was to step back muna. I needed some space to let myself cool down and understand what I was actually feeling instead of saying something while I was still all over the place. And I think that space was good for me.

Pero habang ginagawa ko yun, may isa rin akong napansin nitong buong week. Kahit sinusubukan kong mag-step back and regulate myself, somehow nagkakausap pa rin tayo. And I think that said something too.

And honestly, yung joke natin about "unconfessing" added another layer to what I was already processing. Hindi naman sa kinuha ko siya as something serious, pero dahil sa where I was emotionally, it landed a bit heavier than you probably meant it to.

And I think that's something I don't want to do. I don't want fear to make me suddenly take back what I genuinely feel, or make me act like I don't care just because things got a bit scary or uncertain.

Siguro habang iniisip ko rin kung ano ba talaga yung gusto ko, narealize ko na I don't really want something na puro sweetness or yung kailangan laging may romantic gestures. I think I want something more grounded than that.

I think that's the kind of foundation I'd want to have with someone I genuinely choose to keep getting to know. I don't know exactly what that relationship would look like, and honestly, I don't think I need to figure that out perfectly right now.

And if I'm being honest, I know I want to keep exploring that possibility with you.

That's also why I said I want to pursue you intentionally. Hindi dahil kailangan kong may title tayo, or because I want to rush anything. I just genuinely like that I'm still curious about you and I want to keep being intentional about it.

Yung time natin before sa Megamall is probably one of the reasons why I keep wanting to do that. I really enjoyed that time with you. It felt different from our usual dynamic, and I liked it.

And I also want to acknowledge something about the way I've been inviting you lately. Minsan I get these ideas in my head and I get excited about them, and before I know it, I'm already texting you about it.

Hindi naman talaga yun yung intention ko. I genuinely just wanted to include you in the things I was already thinking of doing. Kaya nga may mga "timezone" invites ako sometimes because the idea itself made me think of you immediately.

Hindi rin dahil iniisip kong kailangan mong magsaya or kailangan kitang ilabas kasi pagod ka. I don't want to decide that for you. Gusto ko lang talaga makasama ka. I wanted to include you because I genuinely wanted to spend that time with you.

For me, that's really what pursuing you intentionally means. It's not about trying to get somewhere as quickly as possible. It's about genuinely wanting to know you, including the parts that might take longer to understand.

At the same time, I know that being honest about what I want doesn't mean I get to decide what you want. You have your own choices, your own pace, and your own boundaries that I need to respect.

That's probably the biggest thing I realized from taking that space this week. I can choose myself without having to become distant from you. I can protect myself without shutting you out completely.

So ayun. Hindi ko sinusulat 'to para kailangan mong sagutin lahat ng sinabi ko. I don't need you to figure everything out right now, and I don't need an answer just because I'm finally saying all this.

I just wanted to let this out properly instead of keeping it in my head and letting my silence speak for me. I wanted you to know why I became distant for a while, what I was feeling, and what I realized through all that.

And after having all that space to think, I'm still in the same place. I still want to pursue you intentionally. I don't regret saying that, and I don't regret caring about you the way I do.

For now, that's really all I wanted to say.

Ayun lang. Sorry ang haba HAHAHA. I think I just needed to finally put this into words instead of letting my brain write the story for me. You don't have to figure everything out right now. I'm okay with taking this one step at a time.

— Emil
